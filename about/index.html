About EnKanakku

EnKanakku is a collection of free calculators, converters and everyday tools designed for Indian users – loans and EMI, GST, gold, home construction, salary, budgets and more.

Our approach

  • Free: no sign-up, no hidden charges.
  • India focused: ₹ formatting, GST slabs, Indian units such as cent, gunta and tola.
  • Private: every calculation runs in your browser. We never receive the numbers you enter.
  • Fast: lightweight pages that work on any phone.

Important note

Our tools give estimates to help you plan. Banks, builders, jewellers and tax rules differ, so always confirm figures with the relevant provider or a qualified professional before making a financial decision.
