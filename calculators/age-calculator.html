How it works

Age is the calendar difference between your date of birth and the chosen date, expressed in years, months and days.

FAQs

How is age calculated for official forms?

Most Indian forms use completed years as on a cut-off date. Enter that date in “Age as on”.

What if I was born on 29 February?

The calculator uses real calendar dates; in non-leap years the birthday falls on 1 March.
