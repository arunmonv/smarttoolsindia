How it works

Break-even units = fixed costs ÷ (selling price − variable cost per unit). Selling price must exceed variable cost, otherwise you never break even.

FAQs

What are fixed costs?

Costs that do not change with sales volume, such as rent, salaries and loan EMIs.

How can I lower my break-even point?

Cut fixed costs, reduce variable costs or raise your selling price.
