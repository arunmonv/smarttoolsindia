Brick Calculator (Number of Bricks for a Wall)

How it works

Wall volume × 500 bricks per m³ (a standard 190×90×90 mm brick with mortar joints). Add wastage for breakage. Mortar and labour are not included.

What this calculator does

It estimates the number of bricks and the brick cost for a wall, after deducting the area of doors and windows and adding allowance for breakage.

Worked example

A 40 ft by 10 ft wall in 4.5 inch brickwork, with 40 sq ft of openings, has a net area of 360 sq ft. With 5% wastage you need about 2,007 bricks, which at ₹8 each costs about ₹16,056.

Tips and limits

  • Bricks sizes vary by region (country bricks, fly-ash bricks, solid and hollow blocks); use the quantity from a known size when you can.
  • Mortar, plastering and labour are separate costs. Use the cement calculator for mortar quantity.

FAQs

How many bricks per square foot?

About 5 to 5.5 bricks per sq ft for a 4.5 inch wall.

How much mortar is needed?

Roughly 25–30% of the wall volume for a 1:6 cement mortar; use the cement calculator for quantities.
