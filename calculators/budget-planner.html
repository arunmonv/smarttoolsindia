How it works

The 50/30/20 rule gives 50% to needs, 30% to wants and 20% to savings. Savings = whatever remains after needs and wants.

FAQs

Is 50/30/20 right for everyone?

It is a starting point. If your rent is high, adjust the percentages and keep savings as high as you can.

What counts as a need?

Rent, groceries, utilities, insurance, transport and minimum loan payments.
