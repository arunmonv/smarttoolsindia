How it works

Loan = on-road price × (1 − down payment %). EMI uses the reducing-balance formula. Vehicle loans are usually 3–7 years; two-wheeler loans often have higher rates.

FAQs

What is on-road price?

Ex-showroom price plus registration, road tax, insurance and other charges.

Is it better to pay a larger down payment?

Yes. A larger down payment lowers the loan, EMI and total interest.
