How it works

Wet volume = length × width × thickness. Dry volume = wet volume × a dry-volume factor (default 1.54). Each material = dry volume × its share of the mix ratio. One 50 kg cement bag is about 0.035 m³ (both values are editable under Advanced assumptions). Add 5–10% for wastage.

FAQs

Which concrete grade for a house slab?

M20 is the common choice for residential slabs and beams; M15 for plain floors and M25 for heavier loads.

Does this include steel?

No. Use the steel calculator for reinforcement weight.
