How it works

Volume = length × width × thickness. Order quantity adds a wastage allowance for spillage and uneven sub-base. One cubic metre equals about 35.31 cubic feet. For the cement, sand and aggregate needed to mix it yourself, use the cement calculator.

FAQs

How much extra concrete should I order?

Add 5–10% to cover spillage and variations in the sub-base.

What is ready-mix concrete?

Concrete mixed at a plant and delivered by transit mixer, priced per cubic metre by grade.
