How it works

The tool counts the whole days between the two dates (the end date is not counted twice) and then breaks the span into years, months and days.

FAQs

Is the end date included?

No. The count is the difference between the dates, so 1 Jan to 2 Jan is 1 day.

Does it handle leap years?

Yes, it uses real calendar dates.
