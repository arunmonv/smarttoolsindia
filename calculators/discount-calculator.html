How it works

Final price = price × (1 − d1 ÷ 100) × (1 − d2 ÷ 100). Two discounts are applied one after the other, so 20% + 10% is a 28% total discount, not 30%.

FAQs

Why is 20% + 10% not 30%?

The second discount applies to the already discounted price.

How do I find the original price?

Divide the sale price by (1 − discount ÷ 100).
