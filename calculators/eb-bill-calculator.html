How it works

The bill is built slab by slab, each slab’s units multiplied by its rate. For consumption up to 500 units, the first 100 units are free for domestic consumers; above 500 units the free units no longer apply. Slab rates are revised periodically, and the defaults are sample rates, so compare with your latest bill and edit them under Advanced assumptions. Fixed charges, other levies and subsidies are not included unless you enter them.

What this calculator does

It estimates a Tamil Nadu domestic electricity bill from the units used in the two-month billing cycle. It applies the free units and the slab rates one slab at a time, so you can see what your usage is likely to cost.

Worked example

For 400 units in a two-month bill, the first 100 units are free, the next 100 cost ₹2.35 each (₹235) and the next 200 cost ₹4.70 each (₹940). The estimate is about ₹1,175, or roughly ₹2.94 per unit on average.

Crossing 500 units is expensive: at 500 units the estimate is ₹1,805, but at 501 units the free units no longer apply and it jumps to about ₹2,038.

Assumptions and limits

  • The default slab rates are sample figures and tariff orders change. Check your latest bill and edit the rates in the Advanced box.
  • Fixed charges, other levies, subsidies and commercial or agricultural tariffs are not included.
  • Your meter reading difference over the billing period gives the units to enter.

FAQs

How often is the EB bill generated?

Domestic consumers in Tamil Nadu are billed every two months, so enter the units for the full two-month cycle.

Why does my bill differ from this estimate?

Slab rates, free-unit rules, fixed charges and adjustment charges change from time to time. Check your latest bill and edit the rates under Advanced assumptions.
