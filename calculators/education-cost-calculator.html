How it works

Future cost = today’s cost × (1 + inflation)^years. Monthly savings are the SIP amount needed to reach that future cost at the expected return. Returns are not guaranteed.

FAQs

What inflation should I assume?

Education costs have often risen faster than general inflation; 8–10% is a cautious assumption.

What if returns are lower?

Lower returns mean you need to save more each month. Try a lower return rate to test.
