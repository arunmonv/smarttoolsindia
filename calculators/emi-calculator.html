How it works

EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n ÷ ((1+r)^n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r the monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n the number of monthly instalments.

FAQs

How is EMI calculated?

EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n ÷ ((1+r)^n − 1), based on loan amount, monthly interest rate and number of months.

Does a longer tenure reduce EMI?

Yes, a longer tenure lowers the monthly EMI but increases the total interest you pay.
