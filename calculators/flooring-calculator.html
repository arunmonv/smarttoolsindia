How it works

Material to buy = area × (1 + wastage %). Material cost uses the buy quantity; labour is charged on the actual floor area.

FAQs

How much wastage should I allow?

5–10% for straight layouts; up to 15% for diagonal or patterned layouts.

Are skirting and polishing included?

No, add them separately.
