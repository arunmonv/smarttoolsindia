How it works

Gold value = weight × rate. Wastage and making charges are added as a percentage, then GST is applied to the gold value (default 3%) and to making charges (default 5%); both rates are editable under Advanced assumptions. Jewellers’ methods vary, so treat this as an estimate.

FAQs

How is gold jewellery price calculated?

Price = (gold value + wastage) + making charges + GST. Gold value is weight multiplied by the day’s rate for your purity (22K or 24K).

What is wastage in gold jewellery?

Wastage is the gold lost while crafting the ornament, charged to the customer as a percentage of weight.
