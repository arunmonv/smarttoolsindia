How it works

Gold value = weight × 24K rate × (carat ÷ 24). Loan = gold value × LTV. RBI caps LTV at 75% for most gold loans (higher for small loans under specific rules). Interest here is simple interest with bullet repayment; your lender’s method may differ.

FAQs

What is LTV in a gold loan?

Loan-to-value is the share of your gold’s value the lender will finance, typically up to 75%.

Which purity is accepted for gold loans?

Most lenders accept 18K to 24K gold jewellery or coins.
