How it works

GST amount = price × rate ÷ 100. To remove GST from an inclusive price, divide by (1 + rate ÷ 100). For intra-state sales the GST is split equally into CGST and SGST.

FAQs

What are the GST slabs in India?

Common GST rates are 5%, 12%, 18% and 28%, with 0.25% and 3% for items such as rough stones and gold. Rates change, so check the latest GST notification for your product.

What is CGST and SGST?

For sales within a state, GST is split equally between the Central (CGST) and State (SGST) governments.
