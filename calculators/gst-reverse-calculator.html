How it works

Price before GST = inclusive price ÷ (1 + GST rate ÷ 100). GST = inclusive price − price before GST. For sales within a state the tax splits equally into CGST and SGST; for inter-state sales the whole amount is IGST. Check the GST rate that applies to your product or service.

What this calculator does

If a price already includes GST, this calculator removes it and shows the original price, the GST amount, and the CGST and SGST halves.

Worked example

A GST-inclusive price of ₹11,800 at 18%: price before GST = 11,800 ÷ 1.18 = ₹10,000. GST is ₹1,800, split into ₹900 CGST and ₹900 SGST.

Common mistakes

  • Taking 18% off the final price gives ₹9,676 instead of the correct ₹10,000, because GST is a percentage of the base, not of the total.
  • For inter-state supplies the full amount is IGST, not CGST plus SGST.

FAQs

Why not just subtract 18%?

GST is added on the base price, so it is 18% of the base price, not of the final price. Subtracting 18% of the final price removes too much.

What are the current GST rates?

Common slabs are 0%, 5% and 18%, with 40% on a few items; 3% applies to gold and silver. Confirm the rate for your item with the GST rate schedule.
