How it works

Loan = price × (1 − down payment %). EMI uses the reducing-balance formula. Under the old tax regime, principal repayment can qualify under Section 80C (up to ₹1.5 lakh) and interest under Section 24(b) (up to ₹2 lakh for a self-occupied home). Tax rules change, so verify the current limits.

FAQs

How much down payment is needed for a home loan?

Lenders typically finance up to 75–90% of the property value, so you need to pay at least 10–25% yourself.

Should I choose a longer tenure?

A longer tenure lowers the EMI but increases total interest. Choose the shortest tenure you can comfortably afford.
