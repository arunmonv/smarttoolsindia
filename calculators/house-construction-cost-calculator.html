How it works

Total cost = area × floors × rate per sq ft. Rates vary by city, specification and finishes (roughly ₹1,500–₹3,000+ per sq ft). The material / labour / other split defaults to a 60/30/10 rule of thumb and is editable under Advanced assumptions; it is not a quote.

FAQs

What is the construction cost per sq ft in India?

It commonly ranges from about ₹1,500 to ₹3,000 per sq ft depending on location, materials and finish quality.

Does this include land, interiors or furniture?

No. This estimates the structure and basic finishing only.
