How it works

Taxable value = quantity × unit price − discount. GST = taxable value × rate. Invoice total = taxable value + GST. This tool calculates the amounts only; it does not create a legal tax invoice.

FAQs

What must a GST invoice contain?

Supplier and buyer details, GSTIN, invoice number and date, HSN/SAC codes, taxable value, GST rate and amount.

Is the discount before GST?

Yes. GST is charged on the value after a discount shown on the invoice.
