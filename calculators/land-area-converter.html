How it works

Every value is converted through square feet: 1 cent = 435.6 sq ft, 1 ground = 2,400 sq ft, 1 guntha = 1,089 sq ft, 1 acre = 43,560 sq ft (100 cents), 1 sq yard = 9 sq ft, 1 sq m = 10.7639 sq ft and 1 hectare = 107,639 sq ft. Local units such as kani, kuzhi or bigha vary by region, so they are not included.

What this calculator does

Plots in India are described in different units depending on the state: cents and grounds in Tamil Nadu, guntha in Maharashtra and Karnataka, acres almost everywhere. This tool converts any one of them into all the others at once.

Worked example

One cent is 435.6 sq ft, about 40.47 sq m and 48.4 sq yards. It is 0.01 acre and about 0.18 ground. A 5-cent plot is therefore 2,178 sq ft.

Tips and limits

  • Check whether a quoted area is plot area or built-up area; the units alone do not tell you.
  • Regional units such as kani, kuzhi, bigha and biswa differ between places and are not included. Confirm them with the local revenue office or your surveyor.

FAQs

How many sq ft is one cent?

One cent is 435.6 sq ft, and 100 cents make one acre.

How many sq ft is one ground?

One ground is 2,400 sq ft in Tamil Nadu and Chennai.
