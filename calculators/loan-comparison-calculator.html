How it works

Each loan’s EMI uses the reducing-balance formula. The cheaper loan overall is the one with lower total interest, though a lower EMI may suit your monthly budget better.

FAQs

Is a lower EMI always better?

Not necessarily. A longer tenure lowers the EMI but usually costs more in total interest.

What else should I compare?

Processing fees, prepayment charges and whether the rate is fixed or floating.
