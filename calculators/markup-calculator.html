How it works

Selling price = cost × (1 + markup ÷ 100). Margin = profit ÷ selling price × 100. A 25% markup gives a 20% margin.

FAQs

Markup vs margin?

Markup is profit over cost; margin is profit over selling price. Margin is always lower than markup for the same profit.

Does this include GST?

No. Add GST separately using the GST calculator.
