How it works

Catering = guests × cost per plate. The subtotal adds every category, and a contingency (default 10%, editable) covers last-minute extras. Costs vary widely by city and season.

FAQs

How much buffer should I keep?

10% is a sensible minimum; 15% if you are booking many vendors.

Where do weddings usually overspend?

Guest count and catering have the biggest impact on the total.
