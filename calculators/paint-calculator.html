How it works

Wall area = 2 × (length + width) × height, minus the area of doors and windows (defaults 20 and 15 sq ft each, editable). Paint = area × coats ÷ coverage. Buy about 5–10% extra for touch-ups. Check the coverage printed on your paint tin.

FAQs

How much area does 1 litre of paint cover?

Most emulsion paints cover roughly 90–120 sq ft per litre per coat, depending on the surface.

How many coats should I apply?

Two coats are standard for walls; a primer coat first gives a better finish.
