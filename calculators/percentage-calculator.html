How it works

A% of B = A ÷ 100 × B. A is what % of B = A ÷ B × 100. Percentage change = (B − A) ÷ |A| × 100; positive means an increase, negative a decrease.

FAQs

How do I calculate a percentage increase?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value and multiply by 100.

How do I find 18% of a number?

Multiply the number by 0.18.
