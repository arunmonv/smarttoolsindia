How it works

EMI uses the standard reducing-balance formula. Processing fees are charged on the loan amount plus GST (default 18%, editable). Personal loans are unsecured, so rates are usually higher than home or car loans.

FAQs

What affects my personal loan interest rate?

Your credit score, income, employer category and the lender’s policy.

Can I prepay a personal loan?

Usually yes, but some lenders charge a foreclosure fee. Check your loan agreement.
