How it works

Each year the deposit is added to the balance and the year’s interest is credited, so the balance grows as (balance + deposit) × (1 + rate). The calculator assumes you deposit before the 5th of April so every year earns a full year’s interest. The PPF rate is set by the government every quarter. Interest and maturity are tax-free.

What this calculator does

The Public Provident Fund is a government-backed savings scheme with a 15-year lock-in. Interest is set by the government, compounded yearly and is tax-free, and deposits qualify for deduction under Section 80C in the old tax regime. The calculator projects the maturity value for a fixed yearly deposit.

Worked example

Depositing the maximum ₹1,50,000 every year for 15 years at 7.1% gives a total deposit of ₹22,50,000 and a maturity value of about ₹40,68,209, of which about ₹18,18,209 is tax-free interest.

Tips and limits

  • Deposit before the 5th of the month if you want that month’s interest; a lump sum before 5 April gives a full year’s interest.
  • The PPF rate is revised every quarter, so the final value will differ from a fixed-rate projection.
  • Deposits above ₹1.5 lakh in a year earn no interest, so the calculator caps the figure at that limit.

FAQs

What is the PPF lock-in?

15 years, extendable in blocks of 5 years with or without further deposits.

What is the maximum PPF deposit?

₹1.5 lakh per financial year. Amounts above this earn no interest and get no deduction.
