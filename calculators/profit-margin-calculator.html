How it works

Profit = selling price − cost. Margin % = profit ÷ selling price × 100. Markup % = profit ÷ cost × 100. Margin is measured against revenue; markup against cost.

FAQs

What is the difference between margin and markup?

Margin is profit as a percentage of the selling price; markup is profit as a percentage of the cost price. A 25% markup equals a 20% margin.

How do I price a product for a target margin?

Selling price = cost ÷ (1 − margin %). For a 20% margin on ₹800 cost, price it at ₹1,000.
