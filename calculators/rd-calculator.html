How it works

Each monthly deposit earns interest, compounded quarterly, for the months left until maturity, and the results are added up. This is the method most Indian banks use. Your bank’s figure can differ by a few rupees because of rounding.

What this calculator does

A recurring deposit lets you deposit a fixed amount every month and earn interest like a fixed deposit. This calculator adds up each instalment with the interest it earns until maturity, using quarterly compounding.

Worked example

Depositing ₹5,000 a month for 5 years at 6.5% means ₹3,00,000 deposited. The maturity amount is about ₹3,54,954, so the interest earned is about ₹54,954.

Tips and limits

  • Pay on time every month; late instalments attract a penalty.
  • Interest is taxable. Your bank’s maturity figure can differ by a few rupees from this estimate because of rounding rules.

FAQs

What is the difference between FD and RD?

An FD is a one-time deposit; an RD is a fixed monthly deposit, which suits people who save from a regular salary.

What if I miss an instalment?

Banks usually charge a small penalty, and repeated misses can close the RD early.
