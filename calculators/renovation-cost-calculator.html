How it works

Base estimate = area × your rate per sq ft (typically about ₹400 for light work such as painting, ₹800 for standard work, ₹1,500 or more for a full remodel). Contingency covers surprises such as hidden damage. The per-sq-ft rates are rough averages; get quotes from local contractors.

FAQs

Why keep a contingency?

Renovations often uncover hidden issues such as damp or wiring problems, so 10–15% extra is prudent.

Are furniture and appliances included?

No, only the renovation work.
