How it works

ROI = (final value − invested) ÷ invested × 100. CAGR = (final ÷ invested)^(1 ÷ years) − 1, which smooths returns into a yearly rate.

FAQs

ROI vs CAGR?

ROI is the total return; CAGR is the equivalent yearly return, so it lets you compare investments held for different periods.

Does this include taxes?

No. Deduct taxes and fees separately.
