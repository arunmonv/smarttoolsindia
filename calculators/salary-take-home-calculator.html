How it works

Take-home = gross − employee PF − income tax − professional tax. Tax = slab tax on (gross − standard deduction − other deductions), nil if taxable income is within the Section 87A limit, plus cess. Slabs shown are the new regime (FY 2025-26) and the old regime; every other figure is editable under Advanced assumptions. It ignores employer-specific items (bonuses, insurance, HRA exemption rules), so your payslip will differ.

FAQs

Is this exact?

No. It is an estimate; your payslip depends on your employer’s salary structure and your declarations.

Which tax regime should I pick?

Try both. For the old regime, enter your 80C, HRA and other deductions under Advanced assumptions and set the standard deduction to ₹50,000 and the 87A limit to ₹5,00,000. Tax slabs change with each Union Budget, so verify against the latest rules.
