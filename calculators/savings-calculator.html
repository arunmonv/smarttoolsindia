How it works

Maturity = lump sum × (1+r)^n + monthly × ((1+r)^n − 1) ÷ r × (1+r), with r the monthly rate and n the number of months. Returns from market-linked investments are not guaranteed.

FAQs

Is the return guaranteed?

No. The figure assumes a constant return; actual market returns vary.

Why does starting early matter?

Compounding works on earlier gains, so the same monthly amount grows far more over a longer period.
