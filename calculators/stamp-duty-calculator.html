How it works

Stamp duty and registration fee are charged as a percentage of the higher of the sale price and the government guideline value. The defaults (7% stamp duty and 4% registration) are the Tamil Nadu rates for a sale deed; rates, concessions (for example for women buyers) and extra levies differ by state and change often, so confirm with your Sub-Registrar office or the state registration portal.

What this calculator does

When you buy property in India you pay stamp duty and a registration fee to the state government on top of the price. This calculator works out both, on the higher of the sale price and the guideline value, and adds them to the price to show your total cost.

Worked example

A flat sold for ₹50,00,000 in an area where the guideline value is ₹55,00,000. Duty is charged on ₹55,00,000. At 7% stamp duty and 4% registration, stamp duty is ₹3,85,000, registration is ₹2,20,000 and the total is ₹6,05,000, making the full cost ₹56,05,000.

Tips and limits

  • Rates differ by state and can include extra levies, surcharges or concessions (for example for women buyers). Change the percentage fields to match your state, and use the Advanced box for other charges.
  • Find the guideline value for your street or survey number on your state’s registration portal.
  • Legal, agent and loan-related fees are separate and not included.

FAQs

Why use the guideline value?

Most states do not allow registration below the guideline (circle) rate, so duty is calculated on whichever is higher.

Are the rates the same in every state?

No. Rates differ by state, property type and buyer category. Change the two percentage fields to match your state.
