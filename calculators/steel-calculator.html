How it works

Weight per metre = d² ÷ 162 (d in mm), derived from steel’s density of 7850 kg/m³. Standard TMT bars come in 12 m lengths. Rates change daily, so use your supplier’s current price.

What this calculator does

Given the bar diameter, length and number of bars, it gives the weight and cost of steel using the d² ÷ 162 formula. It is useful to cross-check a bar bending schedule or a supplier quote.

Worked example

A 12 mm bar weighs 144 ÷ 162 = 0.889 kg per metre, so a 12 m bar weighs about 10.67 kg. For 100 such bars the weight is about 1,067 kg, and at ₹65 per kg the steel costs about ₹69,333.

Tips and limits

  • Real bars vary slightly from the formula within the tolerance allowed by the standard. Add wastage from cutting and overlaps.
  • Rates change daily, so use your supplier’s current price.

FAQs

Why divide by 162?

The formula comes from the cross-sectional area of a round bar multiplied by steel density, simplified for diameters in millimetres.

Which TMT grade is common?

Fe500 and Fe500D are the most common grades for houses in India.
