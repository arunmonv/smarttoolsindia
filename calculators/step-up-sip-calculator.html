How it works

Month by month, the instalment is added at the start of the month and the balance grows by the monthly return. The instalment itself rises by the step-up percentage at the start of each new year. The last result shows a flat SIP of the starting amount for comparison.

What this calculator does

A step-up SIP raises your monthly instalment by a fixed percentage every year, so your investing grows with your salary. The calculator compares the result with a flat SIP of the starting amount so you can see what the step-up adds.

Worked example

Start with ₹5,000 a month and raise it by 10% every year for 10 years at 12% a year. You invest about ₹9,56,245 in total and the estimated maturity value is about ₹16,87,163. The same ₹5,000 flat SIP would grow to about ₹11,61,695, so the yearly step-up adds roughly ₹5.25 lakh.

Tips

  • Match the step-up to your expected pay rise; 5–10% is a common starting point.
  • If a lender or platform steps up by a fixed rupee amount instead of a percentage, the result will differ slightly.

FAQs

What is a step-up SIP?

A SIP whose instalment increases by a fixed percentage or amount every year, so your investment keeps pace with your income.

How much step-up is reasonable?

Many investors choose 5–15% a year, close to their expected salary growth.
