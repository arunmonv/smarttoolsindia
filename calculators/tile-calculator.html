How it works

Tiles = room area (sq in) ÷ tile area (sq in), plus wastage for cuts and breakage (10% is typical; 15% for diagonal layouts).

FAQs

How much extra tile should I buy?

Add 10% for straight layouts and 15% for diagonal or patterned layouts.

Does this include adhesive or labour?

No, only the tiles themselves.
