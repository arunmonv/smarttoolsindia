How it works

Every unit is converted to a base unit (metre, square metre or kilogram) and then to the target unit. Regional land units vary: here 1 cent = 435.6 sq ft, 1 gunta = 1,089 sq ft, 1 ground = 2,400 sq ft and 1 acre = 43,560 sq ft.

FAQs

How many sq ft are in a cent?

One cent is about 435.6 sq ft; 100 cents make one acre.

How many sq ft in an acre?

43,560 sq ft.
