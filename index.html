How to use EnKanakku

Pick a calculator, enter your numbers and read the result straight away – there is nothing to install and no sign-up. Each calculator page explains the formula it uses, shows a worked example and lists the assumptions behind the result, so you can check the logic rather than trust a black box. Fields under “Advanced assumptions” let you change defaults such as GST on making charges, wastage on tiles or the loan-to-value ratio on a gold loan.

Our guides go deeper than the calculators: how EMI is calculated, how gold loan value is decided, how much paint or cement a job needs, and how to plan a monthly budget. They are written in plain language with worked numbers, and they link back to the matching calculator.

All results are estimates for planning. Rates, taxes and material prices change and differ by lender, jeweller, supplier and city, so confirm the final figures with the provider before you commit. See our disclaimer and about page for how the tools are built and maintained.
