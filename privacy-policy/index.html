Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Calculations on EnKanakku run entirely in your browser. The values you enter are not sent to or stored on our servers.

What is stored on your device

We save only your light/dark theme preference in your browser’s local storage.

Analytics and advertising

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how many people visit the site and which pages they view. Google Analytics may use cookies and collects anonymised usage data such as pages viewed, approximate location and device type. The values you enter into calculators are not shared with it. If we add advertising in future, we will update this policy first.
